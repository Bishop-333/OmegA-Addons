Model description       : 

This is Spawn for Quake3Arena, a modification of the Spawn model for UT.

Other info  :      	

After I finished Spawn for UT, I tought that it wouldn't be too much trouble to convert the mdoel to the Q3A format.  So I sliced the models into 3 peices, changed the animations, and wrote my first shader! I haven't been able to reproduce the UT Procedural textures effect (fire, gas) with the shader. But, at least, the animated cloak, chains and the glowing eyes work.
