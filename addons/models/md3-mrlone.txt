Model description       : This is the first model I ever made. 
			  It 's far from perfect, no LOD, clipping, a seam here and there,... 
			  you name it, it has it. But I still like it.
		          I finished it half a year ago, but I always tought I had to improve it before releasing it. 
			  Due to lack of time I only made some small improvements. 
			  Mrlone is the character from a comic I once drew in my sketchbook. 
			  He's supposed to be the final man on earth after some war, 
			  and now he spends his days looking for other survivors.
