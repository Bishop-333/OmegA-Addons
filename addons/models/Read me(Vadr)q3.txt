Model description       : (From the old Darth Vader for Quake2 readme file, I know I'm lazy.)

Formerly Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker, student of Obiwan Kenobi. Seduced by the dark side of the 
Force he became Darth Vader.  As a Dark Lord of the Sith, he was sought to hunt down and destroy 
all Jedi.  Emperor Palpatine ordered him to kill Luke Skywalker, but tried to save the life of 
his son instead.

Other info  :      	

This is the second time I do a model of Darth Vader, I did the first one back in 1999, it was my 
second ppm. Now, this one is my 16th! I decided to do Darth Vader again because I figured out a 
good way of doing the Lightsaber effect. 

After the Unreal Tournament version was finished, I converted the model into the Quake3Arena format.
I really like the way the Environment effect is showing up. The polycount is somewhat high, but the polycount of the Lod1/2 version is very low.
